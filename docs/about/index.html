About Me

I'm an ICT professional and Data Scientist based in Nairobi, Kenya. I build clean, responsive websites with HTML, CSS and JavaScript, and I use Python, machine learning and Tableau to turn raw data into insights people can act on.

I hold a Diploma in ICT from Ramogi Institute of Advanced Technology and graduated in Data Science from Moringa School.

My recent work forecasts sales for a Kenyan baby products retailer, covering the full path from data analysis to an interactive dashboard and a Streamlit app. I enjoy solving real-world problems with technology and I keep improving my skills through hands-on projects.

I'm open to: data science and web development roles, freelance projects, and collaborations.

Right now: I'm building and publishing this portfolio, and writing up my data science projects as case studies, starting with sales forecasting for a Kenyan baby retailer.
