Sales Forecasting for a Kenyan Baby Retailer

Python Time Series SARIMA Streamlit Tableau

The problem

A baby products retailer in Kenya needed to predict future sales so it could plan stock and cash flow.

My approach

I worked with the retailer's monthly sales history. Sales follow a yearly pattern, so the models were tested on their ability to capture a 12-month seasonal cycle.

I compared four forecasting approaches. The best one was chosen on validation months, then scored on separate test months it had not seen.

  • Seasonal-naive, used as the baseline
  • Holt-Winters (additive)
  • Holt-Winters (damped)
  • SARIMA(0,1,1)(0,1,1,12), the selected model

Results

I measured error with WAPE (weighted absolute percentage error), which compares the total forecast error with actual sales. Lower is better.

WAPE (%) by model
ModelValidationTest
Seasonal-naive (baseline)58.5737.90
Holt-Winters (additive)15.2915.18
Holt-Winters (damped)14.8510.62
SARIMA (selected)13.525.57

SARIMA scored 13.52% on validation and 5.57% on the test months, compared with 37.90% for the baseline. That is 85.3% lower error and under the 10% target.

What I would do next

SARIMA scored 13.52% on validation, above the 10% target, and 5.57% on the test months. To confirm the result holds, I would test it over a longer window and retrain it regularly as new months of sales arrive.
