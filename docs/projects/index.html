Sales Forecasting for a Kenyan Baby Retailer

Problem: A baby products retailer in Kenya needed to predict future sales so it could plan stock and cash flow.

What I did: Analyzed the sales data, built forecasting models, and presented the findings as a notebook, slides, a web app and a dashboard.

Result: The selected model, SARIMA(0,1,1)(0,1,1,12), reached a test WAPE of 5.57%, compared with 37.90% for the baseline (85.3% lower error) and under the 10% target
